## 9. Security Safeguards

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss and misuse. Depending on the service and deployment, these measures may include:

- Role-based access controls.
- Tenant separation and tenant-related access restrictions.
- IP allowlisting in configured environments.
- Encryption of designated personal-data fields.
- Authentication and access controls.
- Audit logging and security monitoring.
- Backup and recovery mechanisms.
- Restricted access to production systems and operational environments.

No method of transmission, storage or processing is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that every system, connection, integration or data category is protected by identical safeguards.

The engineering review identified areas requiring improvement or production verification, including migration of certain personal-data encryption from AES-ECB to a stronger authenticated encryption approach, verification and enforcement of database TLS, and reduction of sensitive content in certain audit logs. Accordingly, this Policy does not represent that all database connections enforce TLS or that every data category is encrypted in the same manner.

We will continue to assess and improve security safeguards based on risk, applicable requirements and operational needs.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable law, customer instructions, contractual requirements, dispute resolution, security needs and lawful retention obligations.

Retention may differ across:

- Customer accounts and business records.
- Loan applications and supporting documents.
- Uploaded files and extracted information.
- AI conversations and session records.
- Audio recordings and transcriptions.
- Application, security and audit logs.
- Backups and recovery copies.
- Communications and support records.

**Current implementation limitation:** The engineering review did not establish that a uniform, fully implemented retention schedule or automated deletion process exists for every category of data. Some AI session data, audit records and technical logs may remain stored until the relevant retention and deletion procedures are implemented and executed. Certain logs may also contain information submitted during tool or document-processing calls.

We therefore do not represent that every record is automatically deleted after a fixed number of days.

We are working toward documented retention periods, data minimisation and deletion procedures for relevant data categories. Where a verified retention period applies to a particular service, it should be specified in the applicable customer agreement, product notice or approved retention schedule.

Where deletion is requested, we will assess the request, the applicable legal requirements, the relevant customer relationship and whether retention is necessary or legally permitted. Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove all information from backups, archived records or records that must lawfully be retained.
